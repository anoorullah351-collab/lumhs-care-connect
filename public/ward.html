About & User Guide

LUMHS Care Connect

Ward Management System — Surgery Department, LUMHS Jamshoro

A comprehensive ward management app designed specifically for surgical house officers, medical officers, and registrars at Liaquat University of Medical & Health Sciences, Jamshoro. Built from real ward experience — digitizing and streamlining the daily clinical workflow of a busy surgical unit.

Why this app exists
Managing 30–50 surgical patients on a ward round is overwhelming — paper charts get lost, drug charts are incomplete, and critical tasks fall through the cracks. LUMHS Care Connect puts everything in one place on your phone: patient list, vitals trends, tasks, antibiotics, VTE risk, operative notes, and instant clinical decision support — all syncing live across the team.
What this app does
👤 Patient Management — digital list with diagnosis, bed, consultant, admission date.
💓 Vitals + NEWS2 — color-coded alerts and full vitals history log.
✅ Tasks & Orders — Urgent / Routine / PRN with priority flags.
🧠 Clinical Decision Support — evidence-based guidance for SBP, sepsis, cholecystitis, appendicitis, GI bleed, pancreatitis & more.
💊 Antibiotic Tracker — day counts, indication, OVERDUE alerts for stewardship.
🩸 VTE Risk (Caprini) — auto prophylaxis recommendations.
📝 Operative Note Generator — formatted op note ready to copy.
📚 Past Medical History — one-tap PMH that modifies CDSS guidance.
🧪 Labs & Drug Calculator — record investigations, weight-based dosing.
📄 Follow-up Notes — structured daily progress & discharge planning.
☁️ Live Sync — real-time across the whole team.
How to use it — step by step
1. Sign in & join your ward
Log in with email & password, enter your name & role, type the shared ward code (e.g. LUMHS-SURGERY-A). Everyone on the same code shares one live patient list.
2. Add a patient
Tap + Add Patient on the Ward screen. Enter name, age, bed, diagnosis, consultant.
3. Open a patient record
Tap a card. Use the bottom tabs: Patient · Vitals · Notes · Labs · Reports · Guide.
4. Record vitals
Vitals tab → enter BP, HR, RR, SpO₂, Temp → Save Snapshot. NEWS2 score auto-calculates with color alerts. History is kept.
5. Add antibiotics & drug chart
Patient tab → Antibiotic Tracker → add name, dose, start date, planned duration, indication. App warns when overdue.
6. Use Clinical Guidance
Guide tab → pick a scenario (e.g. SBP) or tap Suggest from current diagnosis. Get immediate actions, antibiotics, investigations & pearls. Final decision is always yours.
7. VTE assessment
Open the Caprini calculator → tick factors → get prophylaxis recommendation.
8. Operative Note
Fill the Op Note template → copy → paste into the file.
9. Tasks & handover
Add tasks with priority. Tap Handover from the Ward screen to generate a full sheet for the next shift.
10. Activity feed
Tap Activity in the bottom nav to see what every team member just did, in real time.
Disclaimer
All clinical decision support is evidence-based and guideline-derived (NICE / SAGES / Sanford / Tokyo / IDSA), but represents assistance only — final clinical decisions rest entirely with the treating physician. Verify drug doses against local formulary and patient-specific factors before administration. This app is not a replacement for the hospital information system.

🔥 Connect Real-Time Sync

This app uses Google Firebase (free) for real-time sync between all team members. Follow these steps once — takes ~5 minutes.

1
Go to console.firebase.google.com → Create a project (name it e.g. "lumhs-ward")
2
In your project → Build → Firestore Database → Create database → Start in Test mode
3
Go to Project Settings → Your apps → Add app → Web (</>) → Register app → Copy the firebaseConfig object
4
Paste the config JSON below and tap Save & Connect
This config is stored in your browser only — never sent anywhere except Firebase.
